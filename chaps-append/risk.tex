\chapter{Safety and Mitigation Procedures}\label{chap:safety}

This section outlines and summarizes the safety report in accordance with the laboratory safety considerations and potential risks applicable during the construction, testing, and operation phases of the project .

\section{Safety}

The project involves the development, assembly, and testing of a small-scale electronic greenhouse system that monitors and controls environmental parameters such as humidity, temperature, light, and water supply. The system makes use of rooftop infrastructure on the M\&M engineering departments solar rooftop laboratory including mains water, solar radiation, and at times grid electricity for component power supply.

\subsection{Type of Work Involved}
\begin{itemize}
	\item Electrical wiring (5V and 12V rails)
	\item Sensor installation (temperature, humidity, soil moisture)
	\item Microcontroller programming and deployment
	\item Use of water lines for irrigation systems
	\item Mechanical assembly of the greenhouse frame and Perspex panels
\end{itemize}

\subsection{General Lab and Rooftop Safety}
\begin{enumerate}
	\item Work within the lab may only be conducted during allocated hours.
	\item Closed shoes must always be worn.
	\item No loose clothing may be worn.
	\item Personal protective equipment (PPE) should be worn.
	\item Good housekeeping practices must be maintained during construction, testing, and operation.
	\item Extreme caution should be taken when working on the sunroof, as the lab houses other projects containing sensitive components.
	\item Safety harnesses must be used when working near roof edges or elevated positions.
	\item The safety report must be visible and accessible while working in the lab.
	\item During operation, ensure that spectators remain within designated safety areas.
	\item Tools and the greenhouse structure must not block roof drains, vents, or access pathways.
	\item Maintain a clear 1-meter perimeter around other ongoing rooftop activities.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Electrical Safety}
\begin{itemize}
	\item All wiring must be insulated, with connections clearly labeled and weather-protected.
	\item Use fused circuits and DC buck converters for voltage regulation (e.g., 12V to 5V).
	\item Only low-voltage systems (≤24V DC) will be operated.
	\item Grid electricity access points must be clearly labeled, and all plugs and sockets must be waterproof.
	\item An emergency stop circuit to disconnect all power sources is mandatory.
	\item Electrical systems must be powered off before performing any maintenance or component adjustments.
	\item Cables leading to and from the greenhouse must not create tripping hazards.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Water Usage and Safety}
\begin{itemize}
	\item Avoid activating the irrigation system when electrical components are exposed.
	\item All water connections must use leak-proof tubing and connectors.
	\item Ensure proper drainage on the roof to avoid slip hazards or standing water near equipment.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Housekeeping and Shutdown}
\begin{itemize}
	\item Remove and return all tools and hardware at the end of each session.
	\item Sweep up debris, zip-tie cables, and label wiring.
	\item Disconnect all power and water connections.
	\item Report damaged components or safety hazards immediately.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Working at Heights}
The working-at-heights safety training was completed on the 2nd of April 2025 by the student. To ensure the safety of all persons involved in the project, proper working-at-heights precautions must always be observed.
