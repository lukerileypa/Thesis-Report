chapter{Evaluation of design}

The design will undergo a structured evaluation process to ensure its functionality and effectiveness. Initially, each sensor will be tested individually to confirm accurate readings and appropriate responses under controlled conditions. Following this, the full system—including sensor inputs, actuator outputs, and control logic—will be tested in an integrated setup over several days to observe its behavior in real greenhouse conditions. This phase will help identify any interaction issues between components and verify that the system maintains stable environmental parameters. Finally, the ultimate measure of effectiveness will be observed in the plant growth over a two-week period, where healthy development will indicate a successful and functional design, while poor outcomes may point to system defects requiring further refinement. The general testing procedures still need to be finalized and this section will be further improved on in the next iterations of this report.
